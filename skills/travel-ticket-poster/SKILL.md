## 风格机制 · Style mechanism

- 把原照放进带半圆缺口和底部圆孔的竖向纪念票根，搭配细长手写标题与低饱和信息区，置于深蓝纹理底上。
- 识别特征：双侧半圆缺口、下方可撕信息区、细长手写标题。
- Medium anchor: A cinematic photographic keepsake ticket with a detachable paper stub, restrained handwriting and carefully cut notches.

10. **composition** — Output portrait 3:4; center one tall keepsake ticket on a subtly textured deep navy-black background.
   输出3:4竖幅，一张纵向纪念票根居中放在低调纹理的深蓝黑底上。
20. **composition** — The ticket's upper region holds the source photograph in a rounded-corner window; the lower region is a separate detachable paper information stub.
   票根上区是圆角原照窗口，下区是独立的可撕纸质信息区。
30. **detail** — Cut one semicircular notch into each side at the photo-stub junction, and six or seven round holes along the bottom edge.
   照片与信息区交接处左右各挖一个半圆缺口，底边排列六至七个圆孔。
40. **texture** — Render fine paper grain, clean cut edges and slight material depth; avoid grime, dramatic aging, curled corners and ornate decoration.
   呈现细纸纹、干净切边和轻微纸厚，不加脏污、重度做旧、卷角或繁饰。
50. **palette** — Retain the original scene and atmosphere with only mild cinematic color grading and fine paper grain; preserve identifiable subjects and the horizon.
   原景只做轻微电影调色和纸颗粒处理，保留主体、天际线及氛围。
60. **typography** — Overlay a slender flowing Chinese handwritten title across open photo space, using dry-brush gaps and light strokes rather than heavy commercial lettering.
   在照片留空处叠细长流动的中文手写标题，笔画有飞白，不用粗重商业字。
70. **palette** — Derive title color from the scene: pale cream for mountain subjects, or restrained coral-pink for coastal subjects.
   标题色取自景物；山野可用浅奶油色，海岸可用克制珊瑚粉。
80. **typography** — Add a very small English italic companion line; keep both title lines away from faces and the principal landmark.
   旁边配极小英文斜体，标题不盖住面孔和主要地标。
90. **typography** — Place one small white mumu sample credit near the photo-stub seam; this is an explicit replacement, not the source creator's signature.
   照片接缝附近只放一处小白字mumu；这明确是样本替换，不沿用原作者签名。
100. **palette** — Use subdued olive-grey paper for mountains or pale salt-white paper for coasts, coordinated with the photograph.
   山景信息区用低饱和橄榄灰绿，海景用盐白或浅青白，与照片协调。
110. **typography** — Left-align a prominent Chinese-and-English place label in the stub, followed by one short Chinese line and one tiny English line.
   信息区左对齐较大的中英地点，下方一短中文和一行很小英文。
120. **typography** — Use only supplied place information; when location is unknown, use a generic scene name instead of inventing a destination.
   仅使用已给地点；未知时用泛称场景，不凭空生成目的地。
130. **typography** — Sample headline: 沿风走向远方; companion: FOLLOW THE WIND; place: 山野 · HIGHLANDS.
   样本主标题为沿风走向远方，英文FOLLOW THE WIND，地点为山野 · HIGHLANDS。
140. **typography** — Sample stub lines: 把此刻收进旅途 and A MOMENT TO CARRY HOME. Add no further text.
   样本信息区短句为把此刻收进旅途及A MOMENT TO CARRY HOME.，不再追加文字。
150. **finish** — Make a collectible artwork, not an official travel document; exclude machine codes, brand marks, application controls and unrelated stamps.
   成品是收藏设计，不是官方出行凭据；不含机器码、品牌、应用控件或无关印章。

## 保留约束 · Preservation constraints

在实际输入观察结果之上应用以下约束；清单不授权凭空添加图片中不存在的元素。

Apply these constraints to observed content; they never authorize inventing absent subjects.

- 照片窗口内保留可辨风景、人、建筑、道路和水面关系。
  Preserve the source's recognizable landscape, people, architecture, roads and water relationships inside the photograph window.
- 仅轻调色并加票根版式，不新增风景或移动主体。
  Only mild photographic grading and ticket framing are allowed; do not add scenery or relocate the principal subject.
- 保持票根结构与指定文字，mumu仅为本次样本明确替换署名。
  Keep the explicit ticket geometry and supplied copy; use mumu only as the declared sample replacement credit.

## 适用范围与几何边界 · Applicability and geometry

- 适用：风景、人像、合影、夜景。
- Applies to: Landscape, Portrait, Group, Night.
- 原照置入纵向票面，允许为版式轻裁空白；不重排主体，侧缺口只出现在连接处。
- Fit the source into the tall photo window, cropping only expendable space; never rearrange subjects or place side notches away from the seam.
- 未明确允许的结构变化均不属于此风格；不把这些方法解释为复刻某位艺术家、品牌或现成作品的授权。
- Structural changes not explicitly allowed above are outside this method. This is not permission to reproduce a named artist, brand or existing artwork.

## 排除项 · Avoid

- 用四周等距邮票齿孔代替两个侧缺口和底部六至七孔。
  Uniform stamp-style perforations around all four edges instead of the two side notches and six-to-seven bottom holes.
- 脏旧纸、繁饰边框、通用粗重商业字、额外乱码或遮住主体。
  Dirty or heavily aged paper, ornate borders, generic bold commercial fonts, illegible extra writing, hidden subjects.
- 二维码、条形码、官方票据宣称、真实交通品牌、应用界面、虚构目的地、额外签名或水印。
  QR codes, barcodes, official ticket claims, real transport logos, application UI, invented destinations, additional signatures or watermarks.

## 验收 · Acceptance

- 3:4深蓝底中央只有一张票根，上照下信息区清楚。
  One ticket sits on a portrait 3:4 navy field, with distinct photograph and stub regions.
- 左右接缝各一个半圆缺口，底部六至七孔。
  Each seam side has one semicircular notch and the bottom has six or seven holes.
- 原照内容未改变，手写主标题与细英文可读。
  The source scene remains intact and the light handwritten title and companion text are legible.
- 地点无虚构，样本署名为mumu，无机器码、官方标志或额外水印。
  Place information is not fabricated; the sample credit is mumu and no machine codes or official marks appear.
