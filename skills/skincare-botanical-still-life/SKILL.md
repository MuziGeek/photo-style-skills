- 识别特征：天然石面布景、少量植物叶片、瓶体结构准确。
- Medium anchor: A calm premium skincare still life with accurate source packaging on natural stone beside restrained botanical accents.

10. **medium** — Create a refined beauty still life around the exact skincare container shown in the reference.
20. **palette** — Use muted botanical green, pale warm stone and the original amber glass without changing product colors.

40. **texture** — Render believable glass transmission, liquid depth, fine reflections and natural stone texture.
50. **lighting** — Use soft diffused directional light with controlled highlights and clean contact shadows.
60. **composition** — Place one complete bottle on a low stone surface with a single modest leafy sprig nearby.
70. **detail** — Keep botanical props secondary and never hide the bottle or invent ingredients, labels or efficacy claims.
80. **finish** — Return one photographic still life without slogans, medical claims, branding or watermark.

## 排除项 · Avoid

- Unrequested people, duplicate subjects or malformed anatomy.
- New logos, watermarks or unreadable pseudo-lettering.
- Clipping the main subject or erasing its identifying features.

## 验收 · Acceptance

- 瓶体、滴管、数量与材质保持准确。
  Bottle, dropper, count and material remain accurate.

  Plants and stone stay restrained without hiding the product.
