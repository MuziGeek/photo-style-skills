# 摄影撕纸海报 · Photography Torn-Paper Poster

以源图配色的左侧撕纸托起真实摄影主体，右侧留白容纳衬线标题与短句，配少量胶带和同源版画纸片。

Anchor a photographic subject on source-colored torn paper, balancing pale space, classical serif copy, translucent tape and a small engraved echo.

## 风格机制 · Style mechanism

- 以源图配色的左侧撕纸托起真实摄影主体，右侧留白容纳衬线标题与短句，配少量胶带和同源版画纸片。
- 识别特征：源图配色撕纸、真实摄影主体、衬线留白排版。
- Medium anchor: A portrait photographic collage on torn dyed paper, balancing an intact realistic subject with pale editorial space and restrained serif typography.

10. **composition** — Make one portrait 2:3 photographic collage poster, viewed straight on as a flat scan, regardless of the source aspect ratio.
   输出一张2:3竖版摄影拼贴海报，以正面平整扫描视角呈现，不默认沿用原图比例。
20. **detail** — For an isolated subject, extract a faithful photographic cutout; for a complete scene, retain essential surroundings inside a torn-edge photograph.
   单体使用忠实的摄影抠图；完整场景保留必要环境，以撕边照片承载。
30. **palette** — Sample dominant, supporting and dark colors from the photograph for dyed paper, accents and lettering; retain a warm-white background.
   从原照提取主色、辅助色和暗色，分别安排染色纸、装饰和文字，背景保持暖白。
40. **composition** — Cover the left 60–68 percent with one large irregular dyed-paper shape; keep the right side pale and spacious.
   左侧60–68%放置一大片不规则染色纸，右侧保持浅色留白。
50. **composition** — Place the photographic subject large at left-center; let small contour sections cross the dyed-paper edge without entering the text area.
   摄影主体在左中部放大，少量轮廓越过染色纸边，但不侵入文字区域。
60. **typography** — Set two short title lines at upper-right in a high-contrast classical serif: upright first line, italic second line.
   右上标题分两行，每行使用短词；采用高对比古典衬线，第一行正体、第二行斜体。
70. **typography** — Choose title words that name visible content or its setting; use supplied wording when available and avoid generic motivational slogans.
   标题指向画面可见的内容或环境；有用户指定文案时优先使用，避免泛泛的励志口号。
80. **typography** — At middle-right, arrange a six-to-twelve-word English observation in three to five left-aligned lines of serif italic.
   右侧中部排一条6–12个英文词的画面相关短句，分3–5行，以衬线斜体左对齐。
90. **detail** — Place one small subject-derived silhouette above that observation and one fine vertical rule to its left.
   短句上方加入一个源自主体的小剪影，左侧配一根细竖线。
100. **texture** — Use warm-white fibrous paper, exposed pale torn cores and soft contact shadows; preserve fine photographic texture within the main subject.
   呈现暖白纤维纸、浅色撕裂纸芯和柔和接触阴影，主图内部仍保留细致摄影质感。
110. **detail** — Add exactly two translucent beige tape pieces: one at the upper-left and one at the lower-right.
   左上和右下各加一条半透明米色胶带，共两条。
120. **detail** — At lower-left, angle a warm-white torn scrap bearing a monochrome vintage engraving interpretation of the same photographed subject.
   左下斜放暖白撕纸，承载同一摄影主体的单色复古版画式小图。
130. **detail** — At lower-right, overlap two or three small coordinated paper scraps, leaving the observation and main photograph unobstructed.
   右下叠放2–3张协调的小纸片，不遮挡短句和摄影主体。
140. **finish** — Apply only light wear; keep the result clean, tactile and legible, with no outer frame, tabletop presentation or watermark.
   只作轻微旧化，保持干净、可触的纸感与文字可读性，不出现外框、桌面陈列或水印。
150. **typography** — Remove unrelated source-layout text; introduce only the two-line title and short observation, with no extra captions, dates, brands or labels.
   移除输入中无关的版式文字；新增文字仅限两行标题和短句，不加说明、日期、品牌或标签。

## 保留约束 · Preservation constraints

在实际输入观察结果之上应用以下约束；清单不授权凭空添加图片中不存在的元素。

Apply these constraints to observed content; they never authorize inventing absent subjects.

- 保留实际主体身份、数量、比例、姿态、连接和物间关系；允许调整海报内的大小与位置。
  Preserve observed subject identity, count, proportions, pose, attachments and relationships; scaling and placement within the poster may change.
- 主图保持真实摄影；场景输入保留必要环境，不把景物拆成无关联抠图。
  Keep the main subject photographic; preserve necessary environmental context for scenes instead of isolating disconnected scenery.
- 仅小剪影和左下版画可复现主体，二者是装饰图形，不是新增的摄影主体。
  Only the small silhouette and lower-left engraving may echo the main subject; these are graphic decorations, not additional photographic subjects.
- 颜色和文案依据实际输入决定，橙花与绿瓶仅属于本示例。
  Derive colors and wording from the actual input; orange flowers and a sage vase belong only to this example.

- 适用：器物、产品、人像、风景、美食。
- Applies to: Object, Product, Portrait, Landscape, Food.
- 实际输出固定为2:3竖版；单体可抠图并等比缩放，场景保留必要环境并加撕边。允许轮廓轻微越过左侧纸边，但不改变身份、姿态、比例和主体关系。小剪影与版画是明确允许的同源装饰。
- Output a portrait 2:3 poster. Isolated subjects may be extracted and uniformly scaled; scenes retain essential context inside torn photo edges. Small contour overlaps do not change identity, pose, proportions or relationships. The silhouette and engraving are permitted source-derived decorations.

## 排除项 · Avoid

- 主图卡通化、人物或物体变形、主体缺失、姿态改变、茎叶断连或任意添加场景。
  Cartoon rendering of the main photograph, distorted bodies or objects, missing subjects, changed poses, disconnected stems or arbitrary new scenery.
- 白色贴纸描边、厚重阴影、粗边框、强烈黄污、过度脏旧、亮面立体样机或带框桌面陈列。
  White sticker outlines, heavy drop shadows, thick borders, strong yellow stains, excessive grime, glossy dimensional mockups or framed tabletop displays.
- 无视原图固定用靛蓝、文字遮挡主体、右侧拥挤或额外营销文案。
  A fixed indigo palette regardless of the source, text covering the subject, crowded right-side space or additional promotional copy.
- 虚构品牌、日期、标志、签名、水印、无关图形，或保留与摄影内容无关的原版式文字。
  Invented brands, dates, logos, signatures, watermarks, unrelated graphics or source-layout text unrelated to the photographed content.

## 验收 · Acceptance

- 输出为2:3竖版，左侧60–68%染色纸与右侧浅色留白明确；主体身份、数量、比例、姿态和连接保留，主图保持摄影细节和必要环境。
  A portrait 2:3 result balances a left 60–68 percent dyed-paper field with pale right space; identity, count, proportions, pose, attachments and necessary photographic context remain intact.
- 纸张、装饰和字体色来自原照，暖白纸有纤维、浅色纸芯和柔和接触阴影；仅轻旧化，不见白描边、重阴影或黄污。
  Paper, accents and lettering follow the source palette; warm-white fibers, pale torn cores and soft contact shadows show only light wear, without white outlines, heavy shadows or yellow grime.
- 右上两行古典衬线标题首正次斜；中右短句6–12个英文词、3–5行左对齐，剪影在上、细竖线在左，无额外文案。
  Two classical serif title lines use upright then italic; a six-to-twelve-word observation spans three to five left-aligned lines below a silhouette and beside a fine rule, without additional copy.
- 两片米色半透明胶带位于左上和右下；左下斜纸承载同源单色版画，右下有2–3片协调纸；无相框、桌面样机或水印。
  Two translucent beige tapes sit at upper-left and lower-right; an angled lower-left engraving scrap and two or three lower-right scraps complete the layout, without frames, tabletop mockups or watermarks.
