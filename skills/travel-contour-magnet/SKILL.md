## 风格机制 · Style mechanism

- 提取旅行主体做成浅色描边的小巧冰箱贴，居中置于同源纯色背景，保留微弱浮雕和柔和投影。
- 识别特征：浅描边轮廓、小巧浅浮雕、纯色留白。
- Medium anchor: A minimal landscape souvenir print with one source-shaped pale-rimmed magnet and a delicate cast shadow on a flat color field.

10. **composition** — Output one landscape 3:2 design with one small souvenir magnet above center and generous clear space around it.
   输出一张3:2横版，在中心稍上方放一枚小巧纪念冰箱贴，周围充分留白。
20. **composition** — Use a single uninterrupted flat background color sampled from the source; include no source-photo panel or lower photographic strip.
   背景为从原照提取的完整纯色，不分出原照面板或下方摄影条带。
30. **detail** — Extract the most recognizable subject or compact scene; preserve its defining silhouette, pose and landmark relationships while simplifying minor detail.
   提取最可辨的主体或紧凑场景，保留轮廓、姿态及地标关系，简化次要细节。
40. **medium** — Give the magnet restrained flat color and crisp simplified contours, without exaggerated cartoon proportions or photorealistic scene depth.
   冰箱贴采用克制平涂与清晰概括线条，不夸张卡通比例，也不保留完整场景的摄影纵深。
50. **texture** — Follow the subject silhouette with a thin pale outline; add subtle shallow relief and a small soft shadow beneath it.
   沿主体外轮廓加浅色细描边，以微浮雕和下方小软影形成厚度。
60. **composition** — Keep the complete magnet small and horizontally centered in the upper portion; its blank surroundings remain the dominant design element.
   完整冰箱贴在上半区水平居中，尺寸小巧，周围空白占主导。
70. **typography** — Place one fine sans-serif English caption below the magnet; use supplied place and time, or neutral theme wording when unavailable.
   下方只放一行纤细无衬线英文；有用户地点时间则使用，否则使用中性主题短语。
80. **typography** — For this sample use exactly COASTAL MEMORY; keep its letters light, discreet and clearly separated from the magnet.
   样本精确使用COASTAL MEMORY，字细且克制，与冰箱贴分开。
90. **finish** — Maintain clear saturated source-derived color without gradients, background texture, elaborate ornaments, multiple badges or heavy shadows.
   颜色从原照提取，干净饱和；不加渐变、底纹、复杂装饰、多枚徽章或厚投影。

## 排除项 · Avoid

- 上照下徽章拼版、下方照片、金色圆形徽章边与过大的主体。
  Photo-plus-badge split layouts, lower photo content, gold circular badge borders and oversized centerpiece emblems.
- 夸张卡通、渐变、纹理底、杂物、文字盖住图标、多枚冰箱贴或厚阴影。
  Exaggerated cartoons, gradients, textured backgrounds, clutter, overlapping captions, multiple magnets and thick shadows.
- 虚构地点日期、品牌、签名或水印。
  Fabricated places or dates, branding, signatures and watermarks.

## 验收 · Acceptance

- 成品为3:2横版纯色背景，没有原照条带。
  The result is a landscape 3:2 solid field without a source-photo strip.
- 主体尺寸小、浅色轮廓和微投影清楚，身份与姿态可辨。
  The small subject has a pale rim and restrained shadow while retaining identity and pose.
- 一行纤细英文在下方，未虚构地点日期。
  One fine English caption sits below without invented places or dates.
- 没有金边圆徽章、背景纹理、渐变或装饰堆积。
  No circular gold badge, background texture, gradient or decorative clutter appears.
