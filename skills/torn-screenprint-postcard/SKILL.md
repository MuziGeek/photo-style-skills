# 撕纸丝印明信片 · Torn Screenprint Postcard

上方原照以纤维撕纸边收口，下方留白中放一幅少色丝印小插画，以低调邮戳和打字机小字连接旅途记忆。

End an upper photograph with a fibrous torn edge, then place a small limited-ink illustration on ivory space with a restrained postmark and typewriter caption.

## 风格机制 · Style mechanism

- 上方原照以纤维撕纸边收口，下方留白中放一幅少色丝印小插画，以低调邮戳和打字机小字连接旅途记忆。
- 识别特征：纤维撕纸边、少色丝印小图、单枚交界邮戳。
- Medium anchor: A torn-edge photographic postcard paired with a small two-to-four-ink screenprint illustration on spacious warm ivory paper.

10. **composition** — Create one portrait 3:4 postcard; the complete recognizable source photograph occupies approximately the upper 55% of the canvas.
   制作3:4竖向明信片，完整可辨的原照约占上方55%。
20. **detail** — Finish the photograph's bottom edge with an irregular natural paper tear, showing clear fine fibers rather than a vector zigzag.
   照片底边为自然不规则手撕纸，清楚露出细纤维，不能用矢量锯齿代替。
30. **composition** — Keep an ivory blank gap immediately below the tear, fully separating the photographic region from the lower illustration.
   撕边正下方留一段象牙白空白，使摄影和下方插画完全分开。
40. **typography** — Place a small vintage typewriter-style subject caption in that gap; identify visible content instead of inventing a destination.
   空白处用很小的复古打字机字写主体名称，描述实际内容，不编造目的地。
50. **medium** — Redraw the defining subject as one small unframed screenprint illustration below, preserving recognizable silhouette while simplifying secondary details.
   下方用无框小幅丝印插画重画可辨主体，保留轮廓，简化次要细节。
60. **composition** — Limit the illustration's height to half the lower region's height; surround it with abundant empty paper on every side.
   小插画高度约占下区高度一半，四周留大量空白。
70. **palette** — Use only two to four restrained low-saturation inks derived from the source photograph; keep the original upper photograph in natural color.
   下图只用从原照提取的两至四种克制低饱和油墨色，上图保持自然照片色。
80. **texture** — Give the lower image subtle ink noise, incomplete transfer and low-fidelity print texture, without watercolor blooms or glossy relief.
   下图带细噪点、轻微漏印和低保真印刷感，不使用水彩晕染或光亮浮雕。
90. **detail** — Place one small worn blue-black circular postmark at the right-hand torn seam, crossing the photograph-paper junction discreetly.
   右侧撕边处一枚小且磨损的蓝黑圆形邮戳，低调压在照片与纸面交界。
100. **detail** — Keep the postmark abstract and unlettered unless authentic text is supplied; do not fabricate a postal date or official authority.
   无真实邮戳文字时用无字抽象邮戳，不虚构邮政日期或官方身份。
110. **typography** — Sample caption: HARBOR AFTERNOON. For other inputs name their visible subject; footer fields stay 地点 · PLACE   日期 · DATE.
   样本小标题HARBOR AFTERNOON；其他输入按可见主体命名，底部保留地点 · PLACE   日期 · DATE通用字段。
120. **texture** — Use warm clean old-paper texture with ample white space; retain a flat printed surface rather than a photographed stationery mockup.
   纸面温暖、干净略旧且留白充足，成品是平面印刷设计，不拍成文具样机。
130. **finish** — Exclude postage stamps, coordinates, exterior borders, curled corners, thick shadows, extra postmarks, duplicate lettering and watermarks.
   禁止邮票、坐标、外边框、卷角、厚投影、额外邮戳、重复文字和水印。

## 保留约束 · Preservation constraints

在实际输入观察结果之上应用以下约束；清单不授权凭空添加图片中不存在的元素。

Apply these constraints to observed content; they never authorize inventing absent subjects.

- 上方照片保留完整可辨的原景、主体与自然氛围。
  The upper photograph preserves the complete recognizable source scene, main subject and natural atmosphere.
- 下图为同一主体的简化插画，保留标志轮廓及主要原色关系。
  The lower illustration depicts the same subject with reduced detail, retaining its characteristic silhouette and main source colors.
- 只允许交界处一枚邮戳，主题文字和通用元数据不宣称未知地点日期。
  Only one seam postmark is allowed; subject captions and generic metadata must not assert unknown location or date.

## 适用范围与几何边界 · Applicability and geometry

- 适用：风景、人像、器物、夜景。
- Applies to: Landscape, Portrait, Object, Night.
- 上照55%、下区45%；下图只占下区约半高，撕边、留白和邮戳分别定位，不把上下两图融合。
- Use 55% photography and 45% lower paper; the illustration uses only half that lower height, with distinct tear, gap and seam postmark.

- Structural changes not explicitly allowed above are outside this method. This is not permission to reproduce a named artist, brand or existing artwork.

## 排除项 · Avoid

- 用矩形细框替代撕边、无分隔留白、下图过大填满下区。
  A thin rectangular photo frame replacing the tear, no separating blank gap, or a large lower illustration filling all remaining space.
- 用水彩代替丝印、超过四色、无关图案、多枚贴纸或色板条。
  Watercolor substituting for screenprint, more than four print colors, unrelated motifs, multiple little stickers or palette swatch strips.
- 邮票、虚构日期、地图坐标、外框、样机透视、卷角、厚影、额外印章或水印。
  Postage stamps, invented dates, map coordinates, outer borders, mockup perspective, curled corners, deep shadows, additional stamps or watermarks.

## 验收 · Acceptance

- 3:4明信片上照约55%，底边有自然纤维撕边。
  The portrait 3:4 postcard has approximately 55% photography ending in a naturally fibrous tear.
- 原照和小插画被象牙白空白明确分开，下图仅约占下区半高。
  An ivory gap separates photo and illustration; the lower image uses only about half its region's height.
- 下图为同源两至四色丝印而非水彩，周围留白充足。
  The same-source illustration uses two to four screenprint inks rather than watercolor, with spacious margins.
- 右侧仅一枚磨损蓝黑邮戳，字少且不伪造地点日期，无边框样机。
  One worn blue-black seam postmark appears at right; sparse copy invents no place or date, and no frame or mockup appears.
