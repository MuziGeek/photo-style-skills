# 水彩爆炸研究图 · Watercolor Exploded Study

上半原照，下半以淡水彩和细墨线画出三至六层悬浮分解结构，搭配引线编号、色板和克制研究排版。

Pair source photography with a three-to-six-layer axonometric exploded study in watercolor and fine ink, supported by numbered leaders and a small palette.

## 风格机制 · Style mechanism

- 上半原照，下半以淡水彩和细墨线画出三至六层悬浮分解结构，搭配引线编号、色板和克制研究排版。
- 识别特征：三至六层悬浮拆解、淡彩细墨线、引线编号与色板。
- Medium anchor: An equal-height photographic and watercolor technical-study diptych, with observed scene structures separated into three to six axonometric floating layers.

10. **composition** — Output one portrait 3:4 sheet with upper and lower regions exactly 50% each; deliver each input as its own complete sheet.
   输出3:4竖幅，上下各严格50%；每张输入单独成一张完整海报。
20. **composition** — Preserve the upper photograph's subjects, geometry, natural lighting and color; fit by modest environment extension, never stretching principal forms.
   上区保留原照主体、几何、自然光和色彩，必要时少量扩展环境，不拉伸主体。
30. **composition** — Center a lower axonometric exploded study, separating the subject vertically or diagonally into three to six evenly spaced floating layers.
   下区中央画等轴测爆炸研究图，将主体沿竖向或斜向分为三至六个等间距悬浮层。
40. **detail** — Retain a recognizable overall structure and at least one open conceptual layer; separate visible terrain or components with clear source correspondence.
   保留可辨的整体结构，至少一层以开放概念切层展示；分离可见地形或部件，各层与原照清楚对应。
50. **detail** — Landscape layers are conceptual terrain slices, not geological evidence; depict only visible structures and never invent underground engineering or unseen interiors.
   风景切层是概念地形层，不是地质证据；仅依据可见结构，不编造地下工程或不可见内部。
60. **medium** — Paint transparent watercolor washes beneath fine ink contours; let pigment and outlines separate slightly instead of using vector-flat fills.
   细墨线下面铺透明水彩，色层与轮廓可轻度分离，不画成矢量纯平涂。
70. **texture** — Keep subject edges precise, surrounding wash edges wet and soft, with granulation and water marks on clean textured ivory paper.
   主体边缘实，环境淡彩边缘虚，干净象牙纹理纸上保留颗粒、水痕与晕染。
80. **palette** — Choose two or three dominant source colors, soften them into warm earth washes, and shade with cool grey rather than solid black.
   提取原照两三种主色，柔化为暖大地淡彩，以冷灰染暗部而非纯黑。
90. **palette** — Retain one source-colored focal accent to anchor recognition; avoid neon saturation, plastic shine and uniformly muddy sepia.
   保留一处接近原照的识别色作重心，避免霓虹、塑料亮面或全图浑浊褐黄。
100. **detail** — Connect layers with fine leaders and hollow sequential numbered circles; give each one short note identifying its visible structural or spatial role.
   用细引线与顺序空心编号圆点连接各层，每层一条短注释说明其可见结构或空间作用。
110. **detail** — Add four to six named color swatches, a modest scale graphic and one archive identifier, kept lighter than the main drawing.
   加四至六块有名字的色板、小比例图形和一处归档编号，视觉比主体轻。
120. **detail** — Label the scale SCHEMATIC / NOT TO SCALE when unmeasured; use a compass only with known orientation, never invented coordinates or dates.
   没有实测时比例图形注明SCHEMATIC / NOT TO SCALE；仅已知朝向才加指北，不编造坐标和日期。
130. **typography** — Use a spaced italic serif study title with a small serif subtitle; annotations use finer low-contrast sans-serif type.
   大号疏字距衬线斜体作研究标题，小衬线副题，注释用更细低对比无衬线。
140. **composition** — Keep title and swatches near a margin; optionally include one tiny complete axonometric view without competing with the central exploded subject.
   标题与色板靠边留白；可有一幅很小完整等轴测辅图，但不与中央爆炸主体竞争。
150. **typography** — Sample title: ALPINE LAKE / LANDSCAPE IN LAYERS; subtitle: A study of visible terrain; archive: NO. R-01.
   样本标题ALPINE LAKE / LANDSCAPE IN LAYERS，副题A study of visible terrain，档案编号NO. R-01。
160. **typography** — Sample layers: mountain ridge, lake, church, village roofs, roads and trees; swatches: STONE / ROOF / LAKE / LEAF.
   样本层对应山脊、湖水、教堂、村落屋顶、道路与树木；色板名为STONE / ROOF / LAKE / LEAF。
165. **typography** — Sample labels apply only to the pictured lake village; for another input derive labels from visible content or use supplied copy.
   样本文字仅适用于这张湖村照片；其他输入须按可见内容重新命名或使用用户提供的文字。
170. **finish** — Keep balanced research-page spacing and visible watercolor warmth; avoid cartoon faces, glossy 3D, pure vector icons and arbitrary doodles.
   保持研究页平衡留白与水彩温度，不使用卡通脸、光亮三维、纯矢量图标或无关涂鸦。

## 保留约束 · Preservation constraints

在实际输入观察结果之上应用以下约束；清单不授权凭空添加图片中不存在的元素。

Apply these constraints to observed content; they never authorize inventing absent subjects.

- 上区摄影忠于原图与可辨配色。
  Keep the upper photograph faithful to the actual source and its identifiable palette.
- 下区允许把可见地形或部件分成概念层，保留可辨对应、相对关系与重要拓扑。
  The lower study may separate visible terrain or components into conceptual layers; retain their recognizable correspondence, relative placement and essential topology.
- 技术标注仅描述可观察或明确概念化的结构，不把虚构测量地点当事实。
  Technical-looking labels describe only observable or explicitly conceptual structure; never present invented measurements or location as facts.

## 适用范围与几何边界 · Applicability and geometry

- 适用：风景、器物、产品、美食。
- Applies to: Landscape, Object, Product, Food.
- 只在下区做概念分层；人、动物或无法判断结构的对象不得凭空剖出内部，改用可见表层或景深切层。
- Conceptual separation occurs only below. For people, animals or unknown structures, use visible surface or depth layers rather than fabricated interiors.
- 未明确允许的结构变化均不属于此风格；不把这些方法解释为复刻某位艺术家、品牌或现成作品的授权。
- Structural changes not explicitly allowed above are outside this method. This is not permission to reproduce a named artist, brand or existing artwork.

## 排除项 · Avoid

- 上下不等高、只做普通水彩转绘，或用蓝底白线蓝图替代水彩。
  Unequal panels, a single ordinary watercolor repaint, or a blueprint replacing watercolor with white lines on blue.
- 无来源额外部件、不可能连接、虚构内部机械、伪实测尺寸或地图坐标。
  Unexplained extra parts, impossible component connections, fabricated internal mechanisms, factual-sounding dimensions or invented map coordinates.
- 不透明矢量色、重纯黑影、脏旧纸、塑料三维、卡通脸、拥挤注释、标志和水印。
  Opaque vector fills, heavy black shadows, muddy aged paper, glossy plastic 3D, cartoon faces, dense annotation clutter, logos and watermarks.

## 验收 · Acceptance

- 3:4海报上下各50%，上照忠实，下图确有三至六个悬浮层。
  The 3:4 poster has equal halves, faithful photography above and three to six floating layers below.
- 各层轴测一致、间距明确，原景整体可辨，开放层为可见结构的概念切片。
  Layers share an axonometric system and clear spacing; the whole scene stays recognizable and open layers are conceptual slices of visible structure.
- 透明水彩、细墨线、冷灰暗部与暖纹理纸共同可见。
  Transparent washes, fine ink, cool-grey shadows and warm textured paper are all visible.
- 空心编号引线、四至六色色板及研究字体层级清楚，技术数据未冒充实测。
  Hollow numbered leaders, four to six swatches and study typography are legible without false measurement claims.
